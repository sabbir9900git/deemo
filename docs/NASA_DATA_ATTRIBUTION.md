# Official NASA & USGS Dataset Attribution

1. **MOLA (Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter)** — NASA Mars Global Surveyor / PDS Geosciences Node (`https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/`, `https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/`). Global 463 m/pixel DEM (`-8,200 m` to `+21,229 m`).
2. **THEMIS (Thermal Emission Imaging System)** — 2001 Mars Odyssey / NASA / Arizona State University (`https://science.nasa.gov/mission/odyssey/science-instruments/`). 100 m/pixel Day/Night IR & Thermal Inertia mosaics.
3. **HiRISE (High Resolution Imaging Science Experiment)** — Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter / University of Arizona LPL (`https://hirise.lpl.arizona.edu/`, `https://www.uahirise.org/hiwish/maps/dtms.jsp`). 0.25–0.32 m/pixel imagery & stereo DTMs.
4. **CTX (Context Camera)** — Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter / Malin Space Science Systems (`https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/`). 6 m/pixel regional context mosaics.
5. **CRISM (Compact Reconnaissance Imaging Spectrometer for Mars)** — Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter / JHU APL / PDS Geosciences Node (`https://ode.rsl.wustl.edu/`). 18 m/pixel visible/near-IR mineral spectroscopy.
6. **SHARAD (Shallow Radar)** — Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter / NASA / ASI (`https://ode.rsl.wustl.edu/`). 15–20 m vertical subsurface radar sounder.
7. **Odyssey GRS (Gamma Ray Spectrometer & Neutron Detectors)** — 2001 Mars Odyssey (`https://ode.rsl.wustl.edu/mars/pagehelp/Content/Missions_Instruments/Mars_Odyssey/GRS/Intro.htm`). Global Water-Equivalent Hydrogen (WEH wt%).
8. **MEDA (Mars Environmental Dynamics Analyzer)** — Mars 2020 Perseverance Rover / NASA PDS Atmospheres Node (`https://pds-atmospheres.nmsu.edu/data_and_services/atmospheres_data/PERSEVERANCE/meda.html`, Bundle `urn:nasa:pds:mars2020_meda::6.0`).
9. **RAD (Radiation Assessment Detector)** — Mars Science Laboratory Curiosity / NASA PDS PPI Node (`https://elib.dlr.de/106090/1/ME-SBA-2016-Reitz_MSL-RAD_ICES_2016.pdf`). Surface dose rate `0.64 ± 0.12 mSv/day` (`0.205 ± 0.04 mGy/day`).
10. **USGS Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature** — IAU / USGS Astrogeology Science Center (`https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/`).
